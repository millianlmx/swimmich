# Swimmich — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Swimmich is a third-party iOS client for self-hosted Immich servers. It is not affiliated with the Immich project. The server you connect to belongs to you or to its administrator, not to the developer of Swimmich.

## No collection by the developer

The developer of Swimmich does not collect, receive, store or sell any data. The developer runs no server and has no access to your photos or to your account.

## No tracking, no analytics

Swimmich contains no tracking, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party SDK of that kind. The app never reads your device's location.

## Data sent to your Immich server, and only to it

The following data leaves your device for the Immich server you chose, and for no one else:

- the server address;
- your email and password when you sign in, or the sign-in with the single sign-on (SSO) provider that your server is configured to use;
- the session token, attached to every request;
- the photos and videos you back up or upload, with their dates and metadata, and any metadata edits you make;
- a random device identifier generated by the app (it is neither a hardware identifier nor an advertising identifier);
- the real-time updates exchanged with the same server.

What the server does with this data is up to its administrator and covered by their own policy.

## Apple services

Maps and the display of places send to Apple the coordinates of your photos and the areas you view: map tiles, place names and routes. AirPlay is handled by iOS. These services are covered by Apple's privacy policy. Nothing goes through the developer.

## Data stored on your device

- **Keychain (this device only):** the session token for each account, the optional PIN of the locked folder, and the session shared with the widget and the share extension (server address, token and device identifier). Your password is never stored.
- **Settings and saved accounts:** the server address, the saved accounts (address, email and name), your user identifier and administrator status, the device identifier, the approved servers, and your preferences (backup and chosen albums, cleanup, language, lock, display).
- **Caches and files:** an image cache on disk, the originals you keep offline, the backup journal, editing states, a cache of map markers (coordinates), downloads and temporary files.
- **App log:** kept in memory only, and exported only if you do it yourself.

This data stays on your device, except what is sent to your server as described above.

Permissions:

- **Photos (read and write):** to back up your library, save photos and free up space.
- **Camera:** only to scan the QR code that configures the server.
- **Face ID:** to lock the app. It is handled by iOS; the app receives no biometric data.

## Account deletion

Swimmich does not create any account. Your account belongs to your Immich server. To delete it, ask the administrator of that server.
